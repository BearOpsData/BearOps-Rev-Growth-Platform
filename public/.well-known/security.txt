# Security Disclosure Policy

If you discover a security vulnerability, please email security@bearops.com
with the details. We appreciate responsible disclosure and will work with you
to address any issues.

# Scope

This policy applies to:
- bearops.com and all subdomains
- BearOps Revenue Growth Platform
- All associated services

# Out of Scope

- Social engineering attacks
- Physical attacks
- Denial of service attacks
- Issues requiring physical access to devices

# Recognition

We recognize security researchers who help us improve our security posture.
